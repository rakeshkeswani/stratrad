## Kite's GTT behaviour (checked 2026-11-09)

Sources: Kite Connect GTT API docs; Zerodha GTT terms; Zerodha support (GTT disabled/cancelled, GTT market orders,
TPIN validity); Kite developer forum, "GTT - Market Protection" (Zerodha staff, 2025).
- Endpoints `POST/GET/PUT/DELETE /gtt/triggers[/:id]`; type `single` or `two-leg` (OCO). The trigger is tracked
  against the **LTP**; when hit, Kite places the order **once**.
- A **limit** order that does not fill that day (e.g. a gap below its price) is **cancelled at day end** -- the lot is
  then unprotected. A GTT can instead place a **market order with market protection** (`market_protection`, raw HTTP
  API; not in every SDK): the unfilled remainder becomes a resting limit at the protection boundary.
- A split or bonus **disables** the GTT; a demerger, rights, consolidation, extraordinary dividend (>2%), series change,
  suspension or delisting **cancels** it. Either way it must be placed again.
- Max **500** pending GTTs per client; valid **365 days**; trigger at least **0.25%** from the price (>Rs 50).
- At trigger time the sell is **rejected** if the shares are not in holdings, or if CDSL authorisation is missing.
  A **TPIN authorisation lasts one day** (7 AM-5 PM); Zerodha recommends **DDPI** for long-standing orders like GTTs.
- Trailing stop-loss GTTs exist on Kite web only, not in the API -- stratrad trails by modifying its GTTs.

## Decided 2026-11-09/10 (Rakesh)

1. **`rakesh_top4` is Rakesh's own strategy, run by him.** His holdings (top-4 included) are `manual` lots that
   stratrad never trades. No stock-average rule engine is needed. stratrad's only strategy candidate is now
   `baseline_momentum` (ST-022 decides whether anything is worth trading live).
2. **Exits for stratrad lots** (baseline, `config/strategies/baseline_momentum.toml`): the per-lot stop (8% initial,
   8% trailing below the highest close) is a GTT; the **12-month time exit** is a daily check inside ST-009 (no
   separate issue).
3. **Ensuring the sale when a stop hits -- three layers** (Claude's recommendation 2026-11-10, after Rakesh asked for
   an approach that ensures the sale; approved with the design 2026-11-10):
   (a) the GTT places a **market sell with market protection** (not a limit), so gap-downs fill; the protection value
   is a setting, chosen from what the API accepts in the dry run against Kite;
   (b) an **intraday backstop** every 15 minutes in market hours: a triggered GTT whose sell is not fully filled gets a
   fresh market-protected sell for the remainder, through `check_before_sell`, plus an alert;
   (c) the **evening reconciliation** alerts on anything still unsold, with the reason.
   Not coverable by any system: a stock at its lower circuit or suspended has no buyers -- alert only.
4. **`check_before_sell`** in the guardrail service, hard checks only (sells are never asked about): the lot exists,
   is open and belongs to a stratrad strategy (never `manual`); quantity <= the lot's and <= Kite's holding; a stop
   modification only moves the stop **up**; CNC, right symbol. One `guardrail_events` row per check.
5. **DDPI is a go-live item.** Without it an automatic GTT sell is rejected unless Rakesh authorised that morning
   (TPIN). Rakesh currently uses TPIN; the choice is his, before go-live. ST-009's build does not depend on it.

## Design (approved 2026-11-10)

Scope: everything from an approved buy to a closed lot, except the approval screen (ST-011).

1. **Broker layer** (`execution/broker.py`): place / cancel orders; GTT create / get / list / modify / delete; read
   orders, trades and holdings; charges via the virtual contract note (`POST /charges/orders`). Order and GTT calls
   go through `KITE_ORDER_PROXY` (ST-007) and only after a guardrail check (the ST-008 test enforces both).
2. **Entry lifecycle**: approved proposal -> `check_before_order` -> buy limit order -> fill -> `positions` row (filled
   quantity, average price) -> GTT for exactly that lot -> `gtt_orders` row -> `order_charges` row. Partial fills:
   the filled part becomes the lot; the rest stays a live order (counted as committed money by ST-008).
3. **Stops**: one GTT per lot, single trigger, market sell with protection. Initial stop from the strategy config.
4. **Trailing**, after the close: new stop = max(current stop, highest close since entry x (1 - trailing)); Kite is
   modified only when the stop rises by at least a setting (default 0.5%), never down.
5. **Time exit**: a lot held more than 12 months is sold on the first trading day after, market-protected, through
   `check_before_sell`.
6. **Intraday backstop** (layer 3b).
7. **Evening reconciliation** -> `reconciliations` + alert: Kite holdings vs all lots per stock; Kite GTTs vs
   `gtt_orders` (any GTT not stratrad's is flagged -- Rakesh keeps none); disabled / cancelled GTTs; after a split or
   bonus, a `position_adjustments` row (quantity, entry price and stop scaled by the factor) and the GTT re-placed;
   GTT count vs the 500 cap.
8. **`manual` lots**: imported from Kite holdings, one lot per stock, quantity = holding minus stratrad's lots,
   recalculated each evening. A negative residual (stratrad's shares sold outside stratrad) alerts.
9. **`daily_snapshots`** each trading day: invested at cost, market value, realised and unrealised P&L net of charges,
   rolling 12-month loss % and tier -- computed with the same function as the backtest's Test 1 (open lots from their
   own buy price; ST-022 decision 1). This is what lifts ST-008's G11.
10. **Schedule** -- stratrad's first n8n workflow: morning (after login) time exits; every 15 minutes 09:15-15:30 the
    backstop; evening (after the close) trailing, reconciliation, `manual` residual, snapshot. Each job fails loudly
    without the day's Kite session (`LoginNeeded`); the GTTs themselves live at Zerodha and protect regardless.
11. **Alerts** go to the log and a health line until ST-011 delivers them to Telegram.

**Done when:**
- A dry run against Kite on prod: one GTT placed far below the market on a held stock (quantity 1, market protection),
  read back, modified upward, refused a downward modify by `check_before_sell`, then deleted -- with a
  `guardrail_events` row for every call.
- With fixtures on dev: an approved proposal leads to a buy order; a fill creates the lot, exactly one active GTT for
  its quantity and stop, and a charges row; a partial fill keeps the remainder as a live order.
- Trailing raises stops and never lowers them; a lot past 12 months is sold the next trading day; a triggered GTT with
  an unfilled remainder gets a fresh sell within one backstop cycle.
- Reconciliation reports zero differences on a clean book, and reports each planted difference: missing GTT, GTT
  quantity != lot, holding < sum of lots, a GTT not stratrad's, a disabled GTT after a split (lot adjusted, GTT
  re-placed), a negative `manual` residual.
- A `daily_snapshots` row each trading day whose loss % equals the backtest's Test 1 function on the same lots; on
  prod, ST-008's `guardrail_check --live` no longer blocks on G11.

**Falsifying clause:** NOT done if, after an evening reconciliation, any open stratrad lot has no active GTT and no
alert was raised; or any stop was ever modified downward; or stratrad sold, or placed a GTT on, a `manual` lot; or any
order or GTT call has no `guardrail_events` row; or a snapshot's loss % differs from the backtest function on the
same lots.

**Go-live items this adds:** DDPI (or a daily TPIN routine Rakesh accepts); the market-protection value from the dry
run; Telegram alerts (ST-011).
